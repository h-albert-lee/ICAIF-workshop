\paragraph{Limitations.}
The corpus is synthetic; realism was checked by \todo{practitioner review / IAA} but no real customer text was used, by design. Tier I is not a legal classification. Korean APIs that require account registration and review (e.g.\ CLOVA Studio) were not evaluated. Encoded evasion is deferred to an adversarial track. Statutory citations reflect Korean law as of September 2026.

\paragraph{Ethics.}
No real personal data was used or generated from real data; all identifiers are checksum-valid but fictitious. The benchmark measures detection, not misuse; identifiability-tier categories are released to help defenders and are not paired with attack prompts. \anonv{}{Model attributions: ``Powered by HyperCLOVA X''; ``Powered by Kanana''.}
