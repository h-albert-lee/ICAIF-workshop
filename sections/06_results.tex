\paragraph{Coverage and context.}
Report L-identifier, L-attribute and I-attribute precision/recall/F1, then stratify by T level and applied operation (Appendix~\ref{app:result-layout}). Compare full/local outputs on identical documents using paired confidence intervals, alongside failure rates and all-mentions recall. Examine realized length, subject count and generator jointly; T levels are not paired rewrites. Missed spans alone do not establish legal or cultural beliefs.

\paragraph{Output reliability.}
Strict scoring includes request-level rejection. Separately, post-hoc itemwise validation retains valid siblings and charges one exact false positive per invalid parsed item; a further stage removes only an entire-response JSON code fence. Neither stage repairs quotes, categories, occurrences or truncation. Character overlap gives partial boundary credit but cannot penalize unanchorable items in character units. These diagnostics retain all gold and do not replace headline scores.
