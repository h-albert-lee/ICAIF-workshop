\paragraph{Which information is recovered?}
Compare precision and recall for L-identifiers, L-attributes and I-attributes, then examine variation levels and applied operations. This distinguishes coverage of familiar identifier patterns from recovery of personal attributes under the same target policy. Missed categories alone do not establish a model's legal or cultural beliefs.

\paragraph{When does wider context help?}
Compare full/local outputs for the same documents and target cores, stratified by subject count and realized length. Interpret changes in exact F1 alongside failure rates, all-mentions recall and hard-negative false positives. A negative full/local difference may reflect missed spans, added false positives or less reliable output; raw-response audits distinguish these possibilities. Report generator and length jointly because their distributions are not independent. The two medium-model additions are exploratory, and poor small-model runs remain in the comparison.
